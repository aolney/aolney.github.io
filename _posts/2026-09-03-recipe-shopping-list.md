During COVID I started meal planning to avoid going to the grocery store more than necessary.

That resulted in [this notebook](https://github.com/aolney/recipe-shopping-list) which creates an organized list of ingredients based on a selection of recipes.

Recently I used AI to convert the notebook to a [web application](https://github.com/aolney/recipe-shopping-list).

You can try it out at http://aolney.github.io/recipe-shopping-list-web
